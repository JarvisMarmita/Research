Problem and it's Background

Noise pollution is one of the most common yet overlooked problems in academic settings. In many schools, students’ loud conversations and unnecessary noises in classrooms and hallways disrupt the learning environment. We, the researchers from the Academy of Saint John ASJ, aim to solve this major problem in our school and hope to provide a solution that can be applied nationwide.This issue has a significant negative impact on students’ learning, especially those seated far from the teacher, as they cannot hear the lessons properly. Moreover, we believe that this problem affects not only our school but also many others worldwide. The situation may have worsened due to the large number of transferring students from other schools this year. Another equally important concern is that excessive noise may irritate teachers and staff, leading to decreased academic performance. Over time, this can even result in hearing problems, particularly for teachers and staff who are exposed daily. Lastly, this problem can cause communication difficulties in classrooms and hallways, making effective interaction challenging.

Negative Effects

  1. Reduces students’ concentration and focus.
  2. Causes stress and fatigue among teachers and learners.
  3. Lowers overall academic performance and productivity.
  4. May harm hearing health and emotional well-being over time.

Goal:

To promote a peaceful, productive, and focused learning environment that supports academic success and well-being.

Purpose of Research

The purpose of our research proposal is to mitigate, or even fully eliminate, the noise pollution caused by students, especially in classrooms and hallways. Our group believes that this issue should not be ignored, as noise greatly affects the quality of education and the overall learning environment. Therefore, we have brought it upon ourselves to do something about it. Many students, even those in private schools, receive subpar education due to the noise pollution caused by the students, and not much is being done. We also believe that this solution can be applicable to all schools nationwide with proper maintenance.

Proposed Methods and Solutions

Method and Flow: This research proposal will use surveys for all students, teachers, staff, and other employees involved, such as security guards or academic coordinators.

Questions to be Answered

1. What data to gather?

What the respondents think the causes of noise pollution are from students and what they think can be possible solutions to it.

2. How?

We would hand out a survey for all students and employees of the school to answer.

3. How would we maintain proper research ethics?

We would follow all of the research ethics in this research proposal by constantly checking if we have broken any.

4. How would you analyze the data?

We will analyze the data by tallying all of the surveys and counting which causes of noise pollution have been repeated, then listing them from greatest to lowest, giving priority to the highest.And taking proper action accordingly.
