Problem and it's Background

Noise pollution, one of the most common yet overloooked and impactful problems in the academic setting. In many schools, students’ loud conversations, and unnecessary noises in classrooms and hallways disrupt the learning environment. So we researchers, from the Academy of Saint JohnASJ, ought to solve this major problem in our school, additionally we hope to provide a solution that is applicable nationwide. This problem has significant negative impact on all of the students learning especially those seated far away from the teacher, as they can't hear the teacher talk properly. Moreover we believe, that this problem plagues not only our school, but most if not all schools worlwide. Also we believe that this problem has escalated ever since there were a large number of transferring students from other schools this year. Another thing that is equally improtant is that it may irritate others, especially the teachers and the staff leading to an overall worse academic performance. Over a long period of time this may end in hearing loss for most, especially the teachers and the staff. Lastly this problem may lead to numerous commucation problems in the classrooms and hallways due to the noise not letting proper communication be viable.

Negative Effects

  1. Reduces students’ concentration and focus.
  2. Causes stress and fatigue among teachers and learners.
  3. Lowers overall academic performance and productivity.
  4. May harm hearing health and emotional well-being over time.

Goal:

To promote a peaceful, productive, and focused learning environment that supports academic success and well-being.
